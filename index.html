TURN A PIC INTO A MACHINE

The showroom loads /models/<name>.glb for each demon and falls back to a placeholder if it's missing. Drop your own 3D models in three steps — every tool below has a free tier.

  1. 1
    Pick an image. Clean, front-three-quarter shot of the car on a simple background — the four renders in /images are ready to use.
  2. 2
    Generate the mesh. Upload it to a free AI image-to-3D tool and wait ~1–2 minutes.
  3. 3
    Download .glb and save it as models/diablo.glb, models/testarossa.glb, models/ultima.glb or models/carrera.glb. Keep it under ~5 MB — decimate in Blender if needed.
